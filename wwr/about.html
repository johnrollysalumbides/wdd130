About Dry Oar

About Dry Oar

We started Dry Oar because we love rivers — the loud, unpredictable, alive kind. Our goal is pretty straightforward: get you out on the water, keep you safe, and make sure you leave with a story worth telling. The name's a bit of a joke, honestly — there's nothing dry about whitewater rafting — but it says something true about how we run trips. No matter how rough the rapids get, our guides stay calm, keep things light, and know exactly what they're doing. We take the river seriously and we take you seriously, but we don't take ourselves too seriously. Around here, it comes down to one thing: ride the rapids, keep your oar dry.

History

Dry Oar didn't start as a business plan — it started as a group of friends who couldn't stay away from the river. What began as weekend trips down local rapids slowly turned into something bigger, as more people wanted in on the fun and asked us to show them the ropes. Somewhere along the way, we realized we'd built more than a hobby — we'd built a crew, a reputation, and eventually a company. Since then, we've guided countless trips, trained guides who've become like family, and watched first-timers turn into regulars. We've kept the same spirit from those early days: no pretense, just good people, good water, and a genuine love for the river.

Adventure Awaits You!

Whether you're chasing your first rapid or your fiftieth, Dry Oar puts you right in the heart of the action. Our guides know these rivers inside and out, and they're just as passionate about keeping you safe as they are about giving you the ride of your life. From the calm stretches where you catch your breath to the roaring whitewater that gets your heart racing, every trip is built around one thing — making sure you leave with a story you can't wait to tell. So grab your gear, gather your crew, and come find out why the river is calling.
